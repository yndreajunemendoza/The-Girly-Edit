My personal reset

Things I want to remember.

A small reminder for the days when school, life, or my own expectations start feeling overwhelming.

✓
Take care of myself.

Rest when I need to, eat properly, move my body, and stop treating rest like something I have to earn.

✓
Focus on what actually matters.

I do not need to do everything at once. I can focus on the priorities that matter right now.

✓
Be proud of small progress.

Passing a difficult subject, learning a new skill, showing up, or simply getting through a hard day all count.

✓
Make time for myself.

Read, travel, dress up, get a drink, take pictures, spend time with people I love, or enjoy a quiet day.

✓
Remember that I am still becoming.

I do not have to have everything figured out yet. I can change, grow, learn, and still be proud of who I am today.
